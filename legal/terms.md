## Terms of use

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

**What FollowAlong is.** FollowAlong is a demo built by Samruddhi Kakade and Unnati Shah for the Meta Global AI
Developer Hackathon (Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026). It plays a how-to video one step at a time and uses your webcam and Meta's
AI models to check each step. The hosted demo runs until about Nov 10, 2026.

**Who can use it.** You must be 18 or older.

**What it's for.** Following hands-on repair and DIY tutorials with clear visual steps, such as wiring a thermostat
backplate with no power connected or swapping a Joy-Con thumbstick. **Don't use it for anything hazardous** (live
mains wiring such as smart switches at 120/230 V, machinery, sharp tools, heat or chemicals). The thermostat tutorial
uses no live power, low voltage only. FollowAlong is not a substitute for an electrician; it can't see your breaker,
so it asks. For the Joy-Con, don't bend or puncture the battery; the tutorial doesn't cover battery replacement. FollowAlong checks whether a
step looks done; it does not check safety, quality or technique, and it can be wrong.

**Your videos.** Only upload videos you have the right to use and share with the AI models that process them. Don't
upload videos of other people without their permission. Uploaded videos are deleted from the hosted demo after 24
hours.

**Fair use of the demo.** The hosted demo has a daily budget and limits per visitor. Don't try to get around them, run
automated traffic, or use FollowAlong to break the law or anyone's rights. We may pause live mode at any time.

**No warranty.** The demo is provided as is, without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for
any loss from using it.
