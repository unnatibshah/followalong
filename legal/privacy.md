## Privacy notice

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

**Who we are.** Samruddhi Kakade and Unnati Shah, the two people who built FollowAlong. Contact: https://github.com/unnatibshah/followalong/issues.

**What we collect and why**

| What | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Still pictures from your camera: every few seconds while the live coach watches you work, when you say or press "check me", and when you ask a question. Point the camera at your work, not your face: whatever is in the picture is sent | To check your step, and answer your question, with Meta's AI models | **Not kept by us.** Sent to Meta and discarded |
| Your voice, only if you turn voice on and only while "Listening" shows | To understand commands and questions with Meta's transcription model | **Not kept by us** (no recordings, no transcripts) |
| Videos you upload | Sent to Meta's video model to turn them into steps | The video and its step plan: 24 hours on the hosted demo, then deleted. Meta's copy expires within 24 hours |
| A log of your session: steps, checks, results, timings, cost, safety confirmations and when you overruled the coach | To run the demo, keep it within budget and measure how well it works | At most 30 days, and until Nov 10, 2026 on the hosted demo. Delete it any time with **Delete my data** below |
| Where you stopped in a lesson, and the coach's language | To pick up where you left off | In your browser only, never sent to us; a week |
| A random cookie (`fa_visitor`) | To apply the per-visitor limits and to keep your session to your browser | 1 day |

We don't collect your name, email or account details (there are no accounts), and we don't use advertising, analytics
or trackers. Our application logs don't contain pictures, audio, what you said or your IP address.

**Who else processes data**
- **Meta** (Meta Model API), in the United States: receives your camera pictures, voice (if on) and uploaded videos
  to run the AI models. We use Meta's Standard mode, which Meta doesn't use to train its models. Meta may keep inputs
  for a time for safety and abuse checks under its own terms. See Meta's [terms](https://dev.meta.ai/legal/terms-of-service) and privacy policy <link>.
- **Your browser's voice** (spoken replies): the coach speaks with the most natural voice your browser
  has in your language. If that's an online voice (Google's in Chrome, Microsoft's "Natural" voices in Edge), the text of the coach's
  reply is sent to Google or Microsoft to be spoken. Never your camera, your voice or your questions. An installed
  on-device voice (e.g. a Premium or Enhanced system voice) keeps it on your device.
- **Render** (hosting), in the United States: runs the hosted demo. Like most hosts, it keeps its own request logs,
  which include IP addresses, under its own policy.

**Your choices**
- You can use replay mode without a camera or microphone.
- You can pause checks or turn voice off in the app at any time, and turn the camera off in your browser (or by leaving the page).
- You can clear the cookie in your browser.
- If you choose another language for the coach, the lesson's text (never your pictures or voice) is sent to Meta once
  to translate it.
- **Delete my data** (the button at the end of this page) deletes, at once, your uploads, your step edits and the
  session logs made in this browser, and the progress saved in it.

**Deletion and questions.** Use **Delete my data** below, or open an issue at https://github.com/unnatibshah/followalong/issues. Our logs aren't linked to
your name: the button finds them through this browser's cookie, and from another browser we can delete a session's log
only if you give us its session ID (shown on the completion screen). Recordings of volunteers who helped us test are
deleted on request, and by Nov 10, 2026 unless the volunteer agreed to publication.

**Children.** FollowAlong is for adults only.

**Changes.** We'll update this notice if what we do changes; the date at the top shows the last change.
